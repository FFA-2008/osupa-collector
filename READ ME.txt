osu!PA replay collector v1.4

WHAT IT IS
  A small script that gathers your osu! replays, plus the map files they were played on,
  into zip files you can send for the osu!PA project.

WHAT IT TAKES
  - your osu!standard replays from osu! lazer and/or osu! stable
  - the .osu files of the maps you have replays on (small text files, no audio, no backgrounds)
  - your answers to the 6 short setup questions it asks (all optional), including your
    screen resolution and refresh rate
  Nothing else: no skins, settings, collections, account data or passwords.
  It only READS your osu! folders and never changes, moves or deletes anything in them.

HOW TO USE
  1. Unzip this folder somewhere (for example your Desktop). Don't run it from inside the zip.
  2. Double-click osupa_collect.bat.
     It first shows how your data is used (full text in DATA USE.txt) and asks you to agree.
     It asks if you play lazer, stable or both: type 1, 2 or 3 respectively and press Enter.
     Then 6 short questions about your setup; press Enter to skip any of them.
     If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
  3. Wait until it says DONE. Big libraries can take a while (it shows progress).
  4. A folder called osupa_upload_<date> opens, and the upload page opens in your browser
     with your osu! name already filled in (sign in with Google). Upload ALL the zip files
     from that folder, unchanged (don't unzip or rename them), then press Submit.
     If the page doesn't open, use this link:
       https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdU7paxKc_2gHt0Nfzv9ODscI2shLW522_G8yPj8fWj23Pv1Q/viewform
     No Google account? Send the zip files to FFA2008 (discord is ffa2008) any other way,
     for example a download link (Dropbox, Google drive, etc.).
  5. Once the page says your answer was recorded, you can delete that folder.

WANT TO CHECK IT FIRST?
  Right-click osupa_collect.ps1 > Open with > Notepad. Everything it does is written there,
  with comments. osupa_collect.bat only starts it.
