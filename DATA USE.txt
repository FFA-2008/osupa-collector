osu!PA - how your data is used (version 2, November 2026)

Plain language, not a legal contract. If anything is unclear, just ask me
(FFA2008, ffa2008 on Discord).

1. WHAT IS COLLECTED
   - your osu!standard replays (they contain your osu! name, play dates, cursor and key data)
   - the .osu map files those replays were played on
   - your setup info: your answers to the Collector's questions
   Nothing else. No passwords, account data, skins, settings or other files.

2. WHAT IT IS FOR
   Training the osu!PA engine: it learns from real replays which parts of maps are hard,
   for whom, and why, to help players improve. osu!PA (free, non-commercial) is the first
   project that uses this engine. The engine may be used for similar projects later.

3. HOW IT GETS TO ME
   You upload the zip files through a Google Form, which needs a Google account. They land
   in my private Google Drive, get moved to my own PC right away, and are then deleted from
   Google Drive. The form shows me the Google account name you upload with.

4. WHO SEES IT
   Only me. Your files are never sold, never shared with anyone, never published and never
   sent to an AI. AI only helps me write the code that analyses the files; the analysis
   itself runs on my own PC.

5. WHAT GETS SHOWN PUBLICLY
   At most numbers that don't identify you (for example "players around rank 10k").
   Anything that names you only with your own OK first.

6. KEEPING AND DELETING
   Your files are kept privately on my own drives for as long as the engine is being
   developed. That can be longer than osu!PA itself, because the engine is meant for more
   than one project.
   Message me (Discord or osu!, ffa2008) any time and all your files get deleted from my
   drives right away, no questions asked. An engine version that was already trained on
   your files keeps what it learned from them, but the next version is trained without them.

7. VOLUNTARY
   Sending is your choice. I never pressure anyone to do anything.

8. NOT OFFICIAL
   osu!PA is a fan project and is not made by or connected to ppy / osu!.
   It only reads files on your own computer; it does not use the osu! website or API.
   (If peppy is reading this: HI PEPPY!!! I can prove I'm not using your API or the osu!
   website if you want me to, just write to me on Discord or Gmail.)
